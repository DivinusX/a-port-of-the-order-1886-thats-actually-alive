# v0.2.56: face spots outdoors (#27) and black faces while shaders compile (#61)

v0.2.56 is v0.2.49 (`6b309755`) plus two GPU fixes. Frame generation (v0.2.50 to v0.2.55) is not
in it; those releases were withdrawn.

All runs: Linux (CachyOS), RTX 5050 Laptop GPU, NVIDIA 620.73.04, CUSA00785, a copy of the
tester's `savedata`, a fresh state folder and cold shader cache per run, the launcher's runtime
command and environment plus `ORDER1886_FRAME_STATS=1`. The tester drove the game to the Chapter I
rooftop opening (Galahad close-ups, outdoors).

## #27: black spots on faces

### Cause

The lighting pass redraws each face against the G-buffer depth with LESS_OR_EQUAL, using a
second vertex shader that reaches the position through half-float data
(`2026-10-22-redraw-depth-bias.md`). The 16-unit constant redraw bias from October covers the
few-ulp disagreement indoors. The disagreement grows with the depth slope, though: outdoors and
on surfaces seen edge-on (a cheek in profile) it exceeds 16 units, the lighting fragments fail
the depth test and stay at the clear colour. The reports fit: RTX 4080 Super and 5090 users see
the spots "only outdoors".

### Evidence

| Run | Build | Redraw bias | Spots on Galahad's face |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fix` | v0.2.49 + #61 change | 16 units | Yes: a dark spot on the cheek (screenshot `CUSA00785_20261124_054818_737.png`), confirmed by the tester |
| `bias512` | v0.2.49 | 512 units (`ORDER1886_REDRAW_DEPTH_BIAS=512`) | None (tester observation) |
| `rc2` | v0.2.56 candidate | 16 units + slope factor 1.0 | None (tester observation, about 19 minutes) |

### Fix

The redraw bias gains a slope-scaled part (`ORDER1886_REDRAW_SLOPE_BIAS`, default 1.0, 0 = off)
with the same sign and the same gating: only draws that test depth, write none and compare
LESS_OR_EQUAL or GREATER_OR_EQUAL, without the game's own polygon offset. A constant 512 was
not shipped: it would also let surfaces just behind a face through, whereas the slope part adds
nothing to triangles facing the camera.

## #61: black faces and late objects after cutscene cuts

### Cause

With async shaders on (the default), a draw whose shader translation or pipeline is still
compiling is skipped (`2026-10-24-async-pipeline-compile.md`). A cutscene cut brings new
materials; until they compile, their draws are missing: faces drawn without their skin lighting
come out black, characters and shadows appear late. The reporter's ROG Ally (Z1 Extreme)
compiles slowly enough for this to last.

### Fix

The GPU thread now waits for the compile, paid from a budget: a 100 ms bucket refilled at a
quarter of real time, so a cut can spend it at once but waiting never takes more than a quarter
of the time over a longer stretch. Whatever does not finish in time is still skipped.
`ORDER1886_COMPILE_WAIT_MS` sets the bucket (0 = the old behaviour). Frame stats report
`N pending draws (M drawn after a compile wait)`.

### Evidence

First 15 s of the Chapter I opening, cold cache:

| Run | Build | Skipped draws | Drawn after a wait | Worst frame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `base` | v0.2.49 | 2876 | - | 745 ms |
| `fix` | v0.2.49 + #61 change | 1391 | 197 | 1773 ms |

Tester: black faces while shaders load in `base` and in run A (v0.2.49 launcher run); none in
`fix` or `rc2`.

Trade-off: skipping is halved, not gone, and the first cuts on a cold cache hitch longer (worst
frame 745 to 1773 ms). Later visits use the shader cache and do not compile.

## Stability

The first candidate run (`rc`) lost the GPU after about a minute of steady 60 FPS play with no
compile activity: NVIDIA Xid 32 (corrupted push buffer) from `ps4:window`, then
`ErrorDeviceLost`. The same laptop logged Xid 31 twice on 23 November with earlier builds. The
second candidate run (`rc2`, same build) ran about 19 minutes without a fault or kernel error.
Not resolved: whether the Xid 32 was the existing intermittent fault.

## Checks

`bash build.sh --test` (Linux, clang) passed on the release branch.
