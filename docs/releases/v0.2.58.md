v0.2.58 is v0.2.57 with the face-spot fix limited to the faces it was made for. Saves and settings in `user/` are kept.

## What's fixed

### Rugs and carpets flickering or popping out at low angles ([#67](https://github.com/DivinusX/a-port-of-the-order-1886-thats-actually-alive/issues/67))

**Cause:** the v0.2.56 fix for black spots on faces ([#27](https://github.com/DivinusX/a-port-of-the-order-1886-thats-actually-alive/issues/27)) gives the lighting pass extra depth room that grows the more side-on a surface is seen. It was applied to every surface. v0.2.57 made it smaller, which fixed the first carpet reported, but rugs elsewhere still flickered and jumped in and out when seen at a low angle.

**Fix:** the extra room now goes only to the player character's face, the only place that needs it. Every other surface (floors, rugs, carpets, windows) is drawn as it was before v0.2.56. The face draws are recognised by the game's own shader IDs, which are the same in every session.

Tested in Chapter I on an RTX 5050: with v0.2.57 rugs flicker and jump at low angles; with v0.2.58 they don't, and the player's face has no black spots. The other v0.2.57 changes (the 33 ms shader wait at cutscene cuts) are unchanged.

## Known issue

**A rug can occasionally turn black for a moment** when it's at the edge of the view, and come back when you look at it. This also happens with the depth-room fix switched off completely, so it's a separate problem, not caused by the #27/#67 fixes. It's being investigated. If you saw it on v0.2.55 or earlier, please say so in #67.

## Verification

Both packages build from the same commit in one release run (see `release-verification.json`); native checks and the portable smoke ran on both, and the Linux smoke re-ran on the finished archive. The fix was played on Windows with an RTX 5050 in a test build from the same source: the redraw log showed the extra depth room on exactly the four face draws and on nothing else, the face had no spots, and rugs no longer flickered.

**Not covered yet:** the face draws were identified in Chapter I only; other characters' faces and later chapters were not checked. AMD GPUs, the ROG Ally and Linux gameplay were not run, and the packaged v0.2.58 was not played before release. Testers can bring back the v0.2.57 behaviour with `ORDER1886_REDRAW_SLOPE_PAIRS=all`.
