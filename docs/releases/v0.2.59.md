v0.2.59 is v0.2.58 with native HDR10 output, HDR settings in the launcher and the in-game menu, a scrollable F1 menu and a reorganized launcher. Saves and settings in `user/` are kept.

## What's new

### HDR10 output ([#66](https://github.com/DivinusX/a-port-of-the-order-1886-thats-actually-alive/issues/66))

The Order was made for SDR: its tone mapping squeezes everything brighter than about six times the scene's normal brightness into plain white. The port now recovers those highlights and shows them brighter on an HDR display, natively, with no RenoDX or ReShade.

- **What gets brighter:** only the highlights the game was compressing: the sun, bright sky, lamps, fire and metal glints. Everything below that (faces, walls, most of the picture) stays exactly as in SDR, and so do the HUD, subtitles, menus and the letterbox bars.
- **Output:** HDR10 (BT.2020 colour, SMPTE ST 2084 / PQ), what HDR TVs and monitors expect.
- **How to turn it on:** turn on **Use HDR** in the Windows display settings, then **Launcher → Graphics → HDR10 output** (or **F1 → Graphics → HDR**; on/off applies at the next launch).
- **Settings:** **Paper white** (how bright normal white is: the HUD, menus and the scene; default 200 nits), **Peak brightness** (set it to your display's peak; default 1000 nits) and **Highlight start** (where highlights start to get brighter; default 80%, which keeps faces exactly as in SDR). In the F1 menu they change the picture while you play.
- On a display without HDR10 the game stays SDR and the F1 row says why.

How it works: after the game's own tonemap, the port rebuilds the game's filmic curve from that frame's values and works out, per pixel, how much brighter it may be. The game's frame itself is not changed. Design and evidence: `docs/order1886-hdr-plan.md`.

### A simpler launcher

- **Six pages instead of seven:** Home, Graphics, Game, Controls, Saves and achievements, System. The Performance page is gone: the frame rate, the F1 menu and the performance overlay are under **Game**; the diagnostic and experimental options are under **System → Advanced**, collapsed.
- **Sections on every page** (Graphics: Display, Image quality, HDR, Effects), and **one line per option** with the full explanation under **More information**.
- Options that only matter with a switch on (the HDR settings, the overlay items, the mods folder) appear under that switch.
- Search results show the page and section of each option. Your settings carry over unchanged.

### F1 menu

- The list scrolls with the mouse wheel and a draggable scroll bar; long descriptions scroll too.

## Please test

- **#66, HDR:** on an HDR TV or monitor, with Use HDR on in Windows. Do highlights look brighter, does the rest look like the normal game, and are the HUD and menus at a comfortable brightness? Please include your display and attach the newest `user/order1886/logs/*-boot.log` (it contains `HDR: HDR10 output on`).
- **Linux HDR:** needs a compositor that offers HDR10 to Vulkan games (for example gamescope with HDR enabled, or KDE Plasma 6 on Wayland). If the log says the display does not offer HDR10, the game runs in SDR.

## Verification

Both packages build from the same commit in one release run (see `release-verification.json`); native checks, the launcher tests and the portable smoke ran on both, and the Linux smoke re-ran on the finished archive. HDR was checked on an RTX 5050 laptop with the SDR preview modes and frame dumps (only real highlights boosted, faces and HUD untouched, no artifacts) and played by the owner on an HDR TV, F1 menu included. The new launcher was reviewed in a test build from the same source.

**Not covered yet:** HDR on AMD GPUs and on Linux was not run. The packaged v0.2.59 was not played before release.
